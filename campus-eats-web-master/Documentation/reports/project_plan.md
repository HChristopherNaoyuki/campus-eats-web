## 1. Project Timeline

The Campus Eats project started on March 6, 2026. Work was split into three
phases, each ending with a formal submission.

**Phase 1: Planning and Part 1 Submission (March 6 to April 23, 2026)**

The first week covered planning and drafting the initial project idea. The
second week was spent producing the project plan. The remainder of the phase
was used to build the website and complete all Part 1 documents ahead of the
April 23 deadline.

**Phase 2: Development and Part 2 Submission (April 24 to May 13, 2026)**

Active website development continued from April 24. Error handling was added
and refined throughout this period. Part 2 assignments were completed and
lecturer feedback from Part 1 was reviewed and incorporated before the
May 13 submission date.

**Phase 3: Polish and Final Submission (May 14 to June 22, 2026)**

The final phase focused on refining the graphical user interface and
refactoring the codebase for clarity and maintainability. All final documents
were completed, and lecturer feedback from Part 2 was reviewed and addressed
before the June 22 deadline.

## 3. Deliverables

The following deliverables were produced across the three project phases.

**Phase 1 Deliverables (Due April 23, 2026):**

The Campus Eats process document covering all functional and non-functional
requirements. The project plan covering timeline, milestones, and deliverables.
Initial website pages and core module structure.

**Phase 2 Deliverables (Due May 13, 2026):**

Expanded website with working authentication, menu browsing, cart, order
placement, vendor management, and administrative functions. Error handling
across all modules. Database installation script (install.sql) and seeding
script (seed.php). Review and incorporation of Part 1 lecturer feedback.

**Phase 3 Deliverables (Due June 22, 2026):**

Refined graphical user interface consistent with the minimalist orange and gray
design. Refactored code-base with improved readability and maintainability.
Complete final documentation set. Review and incorporation of Part 2 lecturer
feedback.
